A see-through teleprompter that sits right under your webcam, so you keep eye contact on calls, demos and videos. It's hidden from screen sharing.

## Install
1. Download **GlassPrompter-Setup-1.0.0.exe** below.
2. Run it. No admin rights needed.
   - Windows may show *"Windows protected your PC"* because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
3. Glass Prompter opens under your camera and lives in the tray.

Requires Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit.

## Highlights
- **Glass overlay:** see-through panel with crisp text. Pacing is set in words per minute, and `[PAUSE]` and `[CUE]` markers work in your script.
- **Hidden from screen share:** Zoom, Teams, Meet, OBS and screenshots don't see it, and a live badge confirms it.
- **Script library:** search and autosave, plus import of .txt, .md and .docx files.
- **Phone remote:** scan a QR code to pair. You can control playback and send or edit scripts from your phone, protected by a PIN, with live updates.
- **Drop folder:** save a file to `Documents\Glass Prompter` from any device through OneDrive and it loads automatically.
- **Global hotkeys:** Ctrl+Alt+Space, Up/Down and more work from any app. Tray icon and settings included.
